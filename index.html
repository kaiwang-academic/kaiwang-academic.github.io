About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics and Finance at the Institute of Economic Studies (IES), Charles University, and a Researcher at the Institute of Information Theory and Automation (UTIA), Czech Academy of Sciences.

My research interests lie at the intersection of Financial Econometrics and Digital Commodities. I focus on high-frequency econometrics, risk transmission factor structures, and contagion between centralized and decentralized trading platforms.

I am also interested in applying machine learning techniques to time-series analysis and limit order book data.

Working Papers

2024 - Present
Native Token as a Factor for Chain-Specific Tokens: A Factor Model and CAPM Analysis

Kai Wang

This paper examines whether platform-native tokens (e.g. ETH, BNB) span the risk premia of chain-specific tokens using CAPM-style and multifactor panel regressions. It utilizes mixed-frequency volatility and liquidity factors (MIDAS-style) with robust Driscoll-Kraay inference.

Work in Progress

2025 - Present
Who Leads on Which Horizon? A Multi-Layer Contagion Map Between DEX and CEX Platform Tokens

Constructing a high-frequency connectedness map across intraday and multi-day horizons using TVP-VAR and frequency-domain FEVD spillover measures to distinguish short- and long-horizon leadership.
